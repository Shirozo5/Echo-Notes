System architecture

What happens behind the scenes.

The system is split into two connected pipelines — one running on the student's phone, the other handling the AI processing in the background.

Mobile app pipeline
Sign in (Firebase Authentication)
Record the meeting
Upload the audio
View the generated chapters
Store & export as PDF (Supabase)
AI pipeline
Audio received by the backend
Transcribed with Whisper
Speakers separated
Transcript sent to Gemini
Chapters & summary generated as PDF
How it works

From conversation to finished document, step by step.

01

Hit record

Open the app on your phone during a supervisor meeting and record the conversation, just like a voice memo.

02

Echo Notes listens and writes it down

The recording is automatically converted into text using Whisper, an AI speech-recognition model — no manual typing required.

03

It figures out who said what

The system separates the conversation by speaker, so the transcript clearly shows what the student said versus what the supervisor said.

04

It drafts the academic document for you

Using that transcript, Gemini generates a structured first draft — such as a literature review, methodology section, or updated proposal — ready to review and export as a PDF.
